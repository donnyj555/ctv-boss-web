Common questions

CTV without the jargon

Is Connected TV the same as traditional television?

No. Both use the TV screen, but CTV is delivered through internet-connected streaming environments and can support digital-style geographic and audience targeting.

Can viewers skip CTV commercials?

Premium in-stream CTV inventory is commonly non-skippable. Inventory rules can vary by publisher and platform, so campaign-specific inventory should be confirmed before making an absolute claim.

Does 95-98% completion mean the advertising produced a lead?

No. Completion tells you the commercial played through. It is a measure of delivery and attention opportunity, not proof of a lead or sale. Business performance should be judged using reach, frequency, site visits, leads, conversions and other campaign goals.

Can CTV target one specific person?

CTV is commonly planned at the household or device-graph level. Exact match methods depend on the platform, audience data and applicable privacy requirements.

How does CTV HOMES retarget after the TV impression?

CTV HOMES currently describes its Multi-Touch campaigns as serving matching display banners back to devices in households exposed to the TV commercial. Those banners provide a clickable follow-up path to a listing or other offer.

Do I need to pay for TV placement just to try CTV HOMES?

No. CTV HOMES currently offers a free trial to create one ad with no credit card. Subscription plans cover ad creation; TV media placement is purchased separately when you are ready to run the campaign.
